# Key Reference Articles & Resources

Essential reading from the 10 experts in this research, organized by theme.

---

## AI Content Production Workflows

1. **"How I Do Content Engineering with Claude Code"** — Ryan Law (Ahrefs)
   https://ahrefs.com/blog/how-i-do-content-engineering-with-claude-code/
   Documents his full Claude Code + 23 custom skill files pipeline that generates publish-ready drafts in 6-12 minutes.

2. **"My Complete AI Content Process for Ahrefs"** — Ryan Law (Ahrefs)
   https://ahrefs.com/blog/my-complete-ai-content-process-for-ahrefs/
   Step-by-step breakdown of how Ahrefs produces content with AI assistance at scale.

3. **"Koray's Agents: Generative AI Agents for Semantic SEO"** — Koray Tugberk Gubur
   https://medium.com/@ktgubur/korays-agents-generative-ai-agents-for-semantic-seo-and-topical-authority-d4b247fac72a
   48 free custom GPT agents for automating topical maps and semantic content networks.

## AI Search & Optimization

4. **"The AI Search Manual"** — Mike King (iPullRank)
   https://ipullrank.com/ai-search-manual
   Comprehensive guide to optimizing content for AI search modes, including passage-level optimization and query fan-out strategies.

5. **"AEO is not SEO 2.0"** — Eli Schwartz
   https://www.productledseo.com/p/aeo-is-not-seo-20
   Argues that AI Engine Optimization is a fundamentally different discipline from traditional SEO, not an evolution of it.

6. **"SEO is a product, AEO is brand"** — Eli Schwartz
   https://www.productledseo.com/p/seo-is-a-product-aeo-is-brand
   Framework for understanding when to optimize for traditional search vs AI search.

## Data & Research

7. **"The State of Content SERPs: A 250,000 Keyword Analysis"** — Glen Allsopp (Detailed.com/Ahrefs)
   https://detailed.com/state-of-content/
   Large-scale analysis of which content types and companies dominate Google search results.

8. **"The Brave New World of SEO"** — Mike King (SEO Week 2025 keynote)
   https://ipullrank.com/seo-week-2025-mike-king
   Covers relevance engineering, AI-mode optimization, and the Google Search leak implications.

## Guardrails & Risks

9. **Google's Spam Policies: Scaled Content Abuse**
   https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies#scaled-content
   The official policy defining what Google considers abusive AI content at scale.

10. **"AI Content Wasn't Good Enough. Now It Is."** — Ryan Law (Ahrefs)
    https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-content-wasnt-good-enough-now-it-is/
    Honest assessment of where AI content has improved and where human oversight remains essential.
